# Scope — Task 5 Social Engineering Attacks Report

Oasis Infobyte Task 5 — Research Report: Social Engineering Attacks

## Assessor
Mahmoud Khaled

## Environment
Research/report task only.

## Deliverable
social_engineering_report.md

## Activity Type
No live phishing, impersonation, attack simulation, credential collection, or testing is performed.

## In Scope
- Define social engineering
- Explain phishing and common phishing types
- Explain pretexting
- Explain baiting
- Explain quid pro quo as a bonus technique
- Include examples or case studies
- Document detection indicators
- Document prevention recommendations
- Include employee awareness training guidance
- Include credible references

## Out of Scope
- Sending phishing emails
- Creating fake login pages
- Collecting credentials
- Impersonating real people or companies
- Testing employees
- Public or third-party targeting
- Live social engineering simulation

## Ethics Statement
This task is research-only and defensive. It does not involve performing social engineering against any person or organization.
